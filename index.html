I'm Siddesh Sule, but everyone calls me Sidd. I'm studying BSc Airlines & Airport Management at Chandigarh University, working towards an MBA in Aviation Management. For my coursework I've built working models of a runway with an ATC tower, runway lighting, cargo handling and a 1:50 Hyperloop pod and tube. I've also completed HEC Paris's Entrepreneurship Strategy course on Coursera.

Outside class, I run a small digital consultancy. I build websites, manage social media and create content for clients in India and abroad, and I've launched my own brand, Doggo Delights. At CU I'm a Technical Coordinator with the DACA Team, where I've managed Fresh Gen Universe and Fresh Gen Fiesta, with Student of the Year up next.

When I'm not working, I'm usually travelling. I've explored Ladakh, Kashmir, Spiti, Meghalaya, Rajasthan, Kerala and Karnataka, and gone further to Finland, Paris, Lisbon and Brazil: husky sledding through snow, paragliding above the clouds, surfing at sunrise. I take a lot of long train journeys across India and photograph almost everything along the way. I share it all on Instagram as @sidd.yar, along with my day-to-day hustle.
